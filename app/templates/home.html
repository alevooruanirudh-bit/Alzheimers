An Explainable Hybrid Digital Twin Framework Using Transformer and XGBoost for Early Alzheimer's Disease Prediction

A tabular FT-Transformer learns a compact representation of each patient's clinical, lifestyle and cognitive profile. That representation is combined with clinically meaningful engineered features and passed to a tuned XGBoost model, which makes the final, calibrated risk estimate. SHAP explains every prediction, and each patient's history is kept in a Digital Twin so risk can be tracked over time and explored with what-if simulation.
