Voice help: hear the questions and answer by speaking

For anyone who finds reading or typing difficult, including poor eyesight. Start voice help goes through every question for you: it reads each one, and after the beep you say the answer. Or use the buttons under any single question: Read aloud only reads it out, and Speak my answer lets you answer it by speaking. Spoken answers work in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Safari; Firefox can read questions aloud but cannot listen.
